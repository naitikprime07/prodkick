Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about prodkick

Is prodkick really free?

Yes! All our quizzes and content are completely free to use. No registration or payment required.

How often are new quizzes added?

We regularly update our question database and add new content. Our questions are sourced from reliable trivia databases.

Can I suggest new quiz categories?

Absolutely! We welcome suggestions for new categories and topics. Use the contact form above to share your ideas.

Do you store my quiz results?

Your quiz results are stored locally in your browser for your personal tracking. We don't collect or store personal data on our servers.

Can I use prodkick for educational purposes?

Yes! Teachers and educators are welcome to use prodkick as a learning tool in their classrooms or training programs.

How can I report a bug or technical issue?

Please use our contact form above and select "Bug Report" as the subject, or email us directly at support@prodkick.site.
