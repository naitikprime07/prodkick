Time Management During Quizzes

David Kim Performance Coach
December 8, 2024 7 min read

Time pressure is one of the biggest challenges in quiz-taking. Even if you know the material well, poor time management can lead to rushed answers, missed questions, and unnecessary stress. Master these time management strategies to optimize your performance and maintain composure during any timed assessment.

Pre-Quiz Time Planning

1. Survey the Entire Quiz

Before answering any questions, take 1-2 minutes to:

  • Count the total number of questions
  • Identify different question types and their point values
  • Note any particularly long or complex questions
  • Calculate roughly how much time to spend per question

2. Create a Time Budget

Allocate your time based on question difficulty and point values:

Sample Time Allocation (60-minute quiz):

  • Survey and planning: 2 minutes
  • Easy questions (40%): 20 minutes
  • Medium questions (40%): 25 minutes
  • Difficult questions (20%): 10 minutes
  • Review and final check: 3 minutes

Strategic Question Ordering

3. The Easy-First Strategy

Start with questions you can answer quickly and confidently:

  • Builds momentum and confidence
  • Secures points early in the quiz
  • Leaves more time for difficult questions
  • Reduces overall stress levels

4. Skip and Return Method

Don't get stuck on difficult questions:

  • If you can't answer within your time limit, mark it and move on
  • Make a quick educated guess if partial credit is possible
  • Return to skipped questions with remaining time
  • Sometimes later questions provide clues for earlier ones

During the Quiz: Time Monitoring

5. Regular Time Checks

Monitor your progress at regular intervals:

  • Check time every 10-15 minutes
  • Compare your progress to your planned schedule
  • Adjust your pace if you're ahead or behind
  • Don't panic if you're slightly behind—stay focused

6. The Two-Minute Rule

For most multiple-choice questions, if you haven't identified the answer within two minutes:

  • Make your best educated guess
  • Mark the question for review
  • Move on to the next question
  • Return if time permits

Dealing with Time Pressure

7. Stress Management Techniques

When you feel time pressure building:

  • Take three deep breaths to center yourself
  • Remind yourself that some points are better than no points
  • Focus on the current question, not the time remaining
  • Use positive self-talk to maintain confidence

8. Emergency Time Strategies

When you're running out of time:

  • Prioritize questions with higher point values
  • Use educated guessing for remaining multiple-choice questions
  • Write brief outlines for essay questions if you can't complete them
  • Don't leave any questions completely blank

Question-Specific Time Management

Multiple Choice Questions

  • Average 1-2 minutes per question
  • Read question and all options before answering
  • Use process of elimination efficiently
  • Don't second-guess unless you have good reason

Short Answer Questions

  • Allocate 3-5 minutes per question
  • Plan your answer briefly before writing
  • Be concise and direct
  • Include key terms and concepts

Essay Questions

  • Spend 25% of time planning, 70% writing, 5% reviewing
  • Create a quick outline before starting
  • Focus on answering the question directly
  • Leave space for additions if time permits

Technology and Tools

9. Using Built-in Timers

Many online quizzes have timers:

  • Keep the timer visible but don't obsess over it
  • Set mental checkpoints (e.g., halfway through by 30 minutes)
  • Use warning notifications wisely

10. Practice with Timed Conditions

Prepare by simulating real quiz conditions:

  • Take practice quizzes under time pressure
  • Use a stopwatch during study sessions
  • Practice the skip-and-return strategy
  • Build familiarity with time constraints

Time Management Checklist

  • Survey the quiz and create a time budget
  • Start with easy questions to build momentum
  • Skip difficult questions and return later
  • Monitor time regularly but don't obsess
  • Use the two-minute rule for stuck questions
  • Stay calm under pressure with breathing techniques
  • Practice time management during preparation

Effective time management during quizzes is a skill that improves with practice. By implementing these strategies consistently, you'll find yourself more relaxed, focused, and successful in timed assessments. Remember, the goal is not to rush through questions, but to allocate your time wisely to maximize your overall performance.
